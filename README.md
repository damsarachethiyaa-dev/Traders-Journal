## 🎯 Overview & Philosophy

Commercial cloud trading journals charge expensive recurring monthly fees, require cloud logins, and transmit your private trading strategies, broker accounts, and ticket numbers to external third-party servers.

**Traders Journal** is designed with a strict **local-first, privacy-by-default** standard:

- 🔒 **100% Local & Private:** Everything runs exclusively on your machine. Your trade tickets, reflection notes, and balance metrics stay in your local folder (`%APPDATA%\Traders Journal`). Zero data leaves your computer.
- ⚡ **Direct MetaTrader 5 Sync:** A small MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor streams real-time deal events, partial closes, and balance updates to the app over your own computer (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`) with &lt;100ms latency.
- 🕒 **System Tray Persistence:** Minimizes silently to the Windows system tray to keep listening for closed trades even while the main window is closed.
- 🔄 **Automatic Updates:** The installed app checks for new versions, downloads them in the background, and installs on "Restart and update".

## 🔌 Connecting MetaTrader 5 (Step-by-Step)

The MT5 connector talks to the app over your own computer only (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    MT5["⚡ MetaTrader 5 Terminal"] -->|"WebRequest (Localhost)"| EA["TradersJournalSync_MT5 (EA)"]
    EA -->|"Live deals"| App["Traders Journal (127.0.0.1:8765)"]
    App -->|"Saved locally"| Storage[("%APPDATA%/Traders Journal")]
    App --> Dashboard["Live Dashboard"]
```

1. **Open Traders Journal:**
   - Launch the app and click **Connections** in the sidebar.
   - Click **Generate Connection Code** to get your private pairing token.
   - Click **Download Connector** to save the ready-made Expert Advisor.

2. **Install the Connector in MT5:**
   - In **MetaTrader 5**, open **File → Open Data Folder**.
   - Go to `MQL5` ➔ `Experts`.
   - Copy the downloaded `TradersJournalSync_MT5` file into `MQL5/Experts`.
   - In MT5, open the **Navigator** (`Ctrl + N`), right-click **Expert Advisors**, and click **Refresh**.

3. **Allow WebRequest in MetaTrader 5:**
   - Open **Tools → Options** (`Ctrl + O`) → **Expert Advisors** tab.
   - Check **"Allow WebRequest for listed URL"**.
   - Click the green `+` and add:
     ```text
     http://127.0.0.1:8765
     ```
   - Click **OK**.

4. **Attach to a Chart and Sync:**
   - Drag `TradersJournalSync_MT5` onto **any chart**.
   - Enter your pairing token in the Inputs tab if asked.
   - Click **OK**. Your trade history and live positions appear on your dashboard right away.

## 🔐 People, Sign-In & Multi-Account Security

Traders Journal is a multi-user workstation that still runs 100% on your machine:

- **Owner Account:** The first time you launch the app, you create the owner profile. No default passwords exist.
- **Strict Session Gate:** The app's local server only answers signed-in sessions, so other programs and browser tabs can't read your data.
- **Complete Profile Isolation:** Each person has their own profile, trade log, risk limits, screenshots, and MT5 pairing.
- **Salted `scrypt` Hashing:** Passwords are never stored in plain text. Five failed attempts in a row slow down further tries.
- **Optional Google Sign-In:** Connect your own Google account in **Settings → Connected Accounts**, with no cloud middleman.

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQ

#### 1. Windows SmartScreen says "Unknown Publisher"
Click **"More info"**, then **"Run anyway"**. Windows shows this for apps without an expensive commercial certificate.

#### 2. The app won't re-open
Traders Journal minimizes to the system tray when you close it. Right-click the **Traders Journal icon** near the clock and choose **Quit**, then open it again.

#### 3. MT5 reports `WebRequest error 4014`
The URL isn't on MetaTrader 5's allowlist yet. Go to `Tools → Options → Expert Advisors`, check *"Allow WebRequest for listed URL"*, and add `http://127.0.0.1:8765`.

#### 4. Where is my trading data stored?
On your PC only, in `%APPDATA%\Traders Journal\data`. Journal notes, screenshots, and account passwords never leave your computer.
