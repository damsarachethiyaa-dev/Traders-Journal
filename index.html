Troubleshooting & FAQ
Direct sync card says “Disconnected”

Press Auto-Connect All MT5 Accounts or Sync Now. If MetaTrader 5 is closed, the PowerShell auto-connector will launch it automatically.

Switching between multiple MT5 accounts

Every account you have logged into on this PC is listed under Your MetaTrader 5 Trading Accounts below. Click View on Dashboard on any account card to inspect that account's performance, or choose All Accounts in the Dashboard toolbar.

“MT5 initialize failed”

Make sure MetaTrader 5 and Traders Journal run at the same Windows privilege level (don’t start only one of them as Administrator), then press Sync Now again.

Port 8765 is busy

Another copy of Traders Journal is already running. Close it from the tray, then start the app again.
